Train zones

Teach the system what motion looks like in each zone of your room:

  1. Tap a zone on the floor plan.
  2. Tap Train and for 30 s walk inside that zone: back and forth, turn around, sit down and stand up.
  3. Repeat for each zone. With 2 trained zones it starts using them.
Wrong zone? Tap the zone you are actually in, then "I'm here" while you move.
Neural network

Learns from your trained zones. It trains here, in your browser, and benchmarks itself against the nearest-neighbors method. Retrain it after adding or correcting zones.

How it decides

It only reports what it measures. Activity: some link fluctuates above its threshold (someone is moving nearby). Recent activity: there was motion a short while ago; it does not claim the person is still there, because WiFi cannot tell someone sitting very still from an empty room. No activity: the chosen time has passed without motion. Moved objects (a chair) trigger nothing, because they stay still afterwards.
